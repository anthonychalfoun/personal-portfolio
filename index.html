CodeBrave

CodeBrave is a Lebanese non-profit organization that focuses on providing Computer Science and AI education to underserved children and youth in Lebanon. Established in 2018, CodeBrave aims to bridge the digital divide by equipping students with essential skills and pathways to opportunity. The organization combines classroom learning with teacher training to ensure sustainable impact, and it has positively impacted over 10,000 learners by developing their skills in coding, robotics, and entrepreneurship. CodeBrave operates through partnerships with schools and community centers, delivering structured programs that foster confidence and problem-solving abilities.
